Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 15 March 2026 | Enterprise: FITWITHMANPREETT

At FITWITHMANPREETT, we value your trust and are fully committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you join our fitness squad or interact with our programs, and how we keep it safe and secure.

1. Information We Collect from You

To personalize your health and wellness routines, we collect specific information that you voluntarily submit through our registration or onboarding forms. This includes:

  • Your name, age, city, and gender.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number and email address for program coordination.
  • Physical stats such as height, weight, and waist measurements.
  • Dietary habits (vegetarian/non-vegetarian preferences), food allergies, and any physical conditions you share with us.

2. Purpose of Using Your Information

We use the details you provide exclusively to deliver the services you purchased. Specifically, this allows us to:

  • Design tailored nutritional and workout roadmaps suited to your goals.
  • Send secure session access links, program reminders, and daily guidance directly via WhatsApp and Telegram.
  • Provide prompt customer assistance when you reach out to our support team.

3. Security & Confidentiality

Your privacy is important to us. All information gathered is stored securely and accessed only for coaching delivery. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal details to outside advertisers.
