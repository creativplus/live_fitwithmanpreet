Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 22 November 2026 | Enterprise: FITWITHMANPREETT

At FITWITHMANPREETT, we aim to provide clear expectations and responsive support for all program enrollments and digital coaching resources.

1. Digital Access & Program Delivery

Because our coaching programs, workout routines, diet plans, and private group invites involve digital delivery and dedicated onboarding support, please review your plan details carefully before completing payment.

2. Refund Eligibility & Window
